# UnTangle

Upload a PDF or text file and ask questions about it.

## How to use

1. Sign in with your credentials.
2. Open the **settings panel** (gear icon) to pick an Ollama model and tune options.
3. Upload a `.pdf` or `.txt` file.
4. Ask questions about the document, or type `/summarize` to get a summary.
5. After uploading, click **Summarize** on the confirmation message to summarize that file.

Your conversations and uploads are saved so you can resume them later.
